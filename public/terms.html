2. Account Registration, Identity Verification & One-Account Policy

2.1 Account Creation Methods

Users may create an account using phone number verification, email and password, or federated OAuth (Google Sign-In). All account credentials are bound to a verified identity.

2.2 Strict One-Person-One-Account Enforcement

Every user or driver is permitted exactly one account tied to a verified phone number, government ID, and unique device. SESE normalizes and hashes identity credentials (NIN, BVN, Driver's License) via SHA-256 to ensure duplicate prevention without storing raw PII. Multiple accounts trigger immediate suspension and automatic fund freeze.

2.3 Driver KYC Requirements

To operate as an independent driver partner, you must complete full KYC verification, including:

  • Valid, unexpired Driver's License issued by official government authorities.
  • Vehicle Registration, Roadworthiness Certificate, and valid Motor Insurance coverage.
  • Real-time biometric facial liveness verification matching submitted identity documents.
  • Active telephone number and verified account profile.

2.4 Anti-Spoofing & Platform Security

GPS spoofing, mock location software, virtual emulators, rooted devices, or modified app binaries are strictly prohibited and result in permanent device bans.

3. Ride-Hailing & Synchronized Fare Bidding

SESE is a technology intermediary platform connecting riders with independent third-party driver partners. SESE does not own vehicles or operate direct transport services.

On select routes, SESE provides a transparent Synchronized Bidding System: drivers may accept published base fares or submit counter-bids; riders receive real-time bids with live countdown timers and may accept, decline, or counter-offer. Once accepted, the agreed fare is fixed and legally binding for that trip.

4. On-Demand Logistics & Courier Delivery

Customers sending parcels must ensure items are legally transportable and properly packaged. Strictly prohibited items include illegal narcotics, unregistered firearms, hazardous materials, counterfeit currency, and stolen property. Driver partners have the absolute right to inspect any package exterior and refuse transit on suspicion of prohibited contents.

High-value and courier deliveries are secured with an automated 4-digit Delivery PIN sent directly to the recipient. Drivers must collect and verify this PIN upon delivery before completion is acknowledged on the platform.

5. Pricing & Direct Order Payments

All fares, commissions, waiting fees, and trip settlements are computed strictly by SESE's authoritative backend servers. Client applications render only verified server-calculated amounts.

Customer trips are paid directly per booking via Cash or online Card (Paystack checkout). When paid online, payment is authorized directly through Paystack's PCI-DSS compliant checkout until trip completion. SESE does not operate a stored-value customer wallet, customer deposits, or withdrawal system. SESE never stores raw card PAN or CVV details. If an online payment declines, the customer must select Cash or retry before trip execution.

6. Cancellation Rules, Waiting Fees & In-Progress Cancellation Lock

Riders may cancel free of charge before a driver arrives at the pickup point. If a driver waits beyond the Grace Period (default: 10 minutes), an automated Waiting Fee (default: ₦20 per minute) accrues dynamically as configured by platform administrators.

Once a ride starts (status: IN_PROGRESS or pending_customer_approval), in-app cancellation is strictly locked for both customer and driver. In genuine emergencies, a SESE Hub Support administrator may cancel the trip. Hub administrators retain the authority to cancel any trip regardless of status and manually settle driver waiting compensation.

7. Cash Remittance & Driver Sese Credits

SESE provides Driver Sese Credits strictly as non-withdrawable platform service credits used to settle trip commissions. Sese Credits carry zero cash value and cannot be withdrawn, transferred, or cashed out.

Drivers collecting cash fares retain the full customer fare and remit platform commission via their Sese Credits balance. Drivers purchase Sese Credits via Paystack checkout. Dispatches are temporarily paused if credits fall below the minimum threshold until topped up.

8. Driver Dispatch, Commissions & Navigation

Driver partner orders carry standard base commission rates as configured by platform administrators and settled via non-withdrawable Sese Credits.

External Navigation Notice: SESE does not provide in-app turn-by-turn voice navigation. The platform provides live trip map polyline tracking and a dedicated direct launcher for the driver's preferred external navigation application (e.g., Google Maps, Apple Maps, Waze) on demand.

9. In-App Calling & Chat Standards

  • In-App Voice Calling: Powered by WebRTC peer-to-peer encrypted audio with automatic Agora RTC failover. Calls operate without exposing private phone numbers. Provider switchovers occur silently.
  • Trip Chat: Restricted to text and image attachments only. Voice notes are not permitted in trip chat.
  • Support Chat: Allows text, images, and voice notes (maximum 5MB). Supported audio formats: AAC, MP3, OGG, Opus, WAV, M4A.
  • Prohibited Conduct: Abusive language, harassment, discriminatory remarks, solicitation of off-platform transactions, or threats result in immediate account suspension and referral to law enforcement.

10. Safety, Telemetry & SOS Emergency Tools

SESE continuously monitors route anomalies, geofence violations, and speed irregularities. An in-app SOS Panic Button dispatches live GPS coordinates to saved contacts and SESE Hub safety coordinators. During acute life-safety emergencies, users must contact local law enforcement (112/199) and emergency services directly.

11. Driver Presence, Background Operation & Overlay Permissions

  • Android: The Driver Quick-Return Bubble (floating overlay) is displayed only when a driver is actively online or has an active trip and the app is backgrounded. This requires SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW permission, requested exclusively after an explicit prominent disclosure and consent flow. Drivers may toggle this off at any time in Driver Settings.
  • iOS: Background presence is maintained via APNs Time-Sensitive notifications and Live Activities. No overlay permissions are requested on iOS, ensuring full Apple App Store Guideline 2.5.1 compliance.

13. Limitation of Liability

SESE is not liable for consequential, indirect, or punitive damages arising from service unavailability, route deviation, or driver conduct beyond its reasonable control.
