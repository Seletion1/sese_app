4.1 Delivery Standards & Prohibited Items

Customers sending parcels via SESE delivery must ensure items are legally transportable, properly packaged, and sealed. The following items are strictly prohibited:

  • Illegal narcotics, prescription drugs without authorized documentation, or psychotropic substances.
  • Unregistered firearms, ammunition, fireworks, weapons, or explosives.
  • Hazardous biological materials, toxic chemicals, or corrosive liquids.
  • Counterfeit currency, stolen property, or contraband.
  • Live animals or human remains.

Driver partners have the absolute right to inspect the exterior of any package and refuse transit if prohibited items or illegal contents are suspected.

4.2 Delivery Verification PIN

High-value and courier deliveries are secured with an automated 4-digit Delivery PIN delivered directly to the designated recipient. Drivers must collect and verify this PIN upon delivery before completion is acknowledged on the platform.

5.1 Server-Authoritative Fares

All fares, commissions, and trip settlement amounts are computed strictly by SESE's authoritative backend servers. Client applications render only verified server-calculated amounts.

6. Cancellation Rules, Waiting Fees & In-Progress Cancellation Lock

6.1 Cancellation Prior to Trip Start

Customers may cancel a ride request free of charge before a driver partner has arrived at the pickup location. If a driver partner arrives at the designated pickup and waits beyond the configured Grace Waiting Window (default: 10 minutes), an automated Waiting Fee (default: ₦20 per minute) accrues dynamically.

6.2 Strict In-Progress Cancellation Lock

Once a driver partner picks up the passenger or parcel and initiates the ride (status: IN_PROGRESS or pending_customer_approval):

  • Normal in-app cancellation by either customer or driver is strictly locked.
  • The trip must proceed to completion to ensure verified settlement, passenger safety, and fair compensation.

6.3 Support-Initiated Emergency Trip Removal

If a ride cannot proceed due to mechanical breakdown, medical emergency, or driver failure to navigate to drop-off, a SESE Hub support administrator may intervene via authorized console actions:

  • The customer's trip fare is refunded cleanly to their wallet balance.
  • Driver trip fare compensation is forfeited, except where verified pre-departure waiting fees apply.
