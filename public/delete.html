Account & Personal Data Deletion Request

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policies, the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA), and global data privacy standards (GDPR), SESE Technology LTD provides full autonomy over your personal information. You can permanently delete your account and associated personal data at any time.

Two Ways to Delete Your Account

Whether you still have the SESE Ride app installed or have already removed it from your mobile device, you can initiate a complete account deletion request through either channel below:

Option 1: In-App Deletion (Immediate)

If you have access to your SESE Ride app on Android or iOS:

  1. Open SESE Ride and ensure you are signed in.
  2. Tap the profile menu → Settings.
  3. Scroll to Account & Security → Tap Delete Account.
  4. Complete the security re-authentication prompt (Password or Google verification).
  5. Confirm the prompt. Your active sessions will be terminated immediately.

Option 2: Web Request (No App Needed)

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot sign in:

  1. Fill out the verified web deletion form below.
  2. Provide the registered phone number or email linked to your account.
  3. Our security desk verifies your identity and initiates account deletion within 24–48 business hours.

What Data Is Deleted vs. Retained

When you delete your account, SESE executes a secure, irreversible data erasure pipeline:

Category Data Types Action & Timeline
Permanently Deleted Profile name, profile picture, login credentials, saved home/work locations, contact references, push notification tokens. Purged immediately upon deletion execution.
De-Identified & Hashed National ID hashes (NIN/BVN), phone number hashes, vehicle verification logs. Kept as one-way cryptographic hashes to prevent fraudulent multi-account creation.
Statutory Regulatory Retention Completed trip receipts, payment references, commission ledger entries, dispute records. Held in restricted 30-day regulatory soft-archive, then permanently purged or anonymized for financial tax compliance.

Mandatory Prerequisites for Deletion

To protect your funds and prevent fraudulent abandonment of active rides:

  • Zero Wallet Balance: Your in-app wallet balance must be exactly ₦0.00. Please withdraw or exhaust any remaining balance before requesting deletion.
  • No Active Rides or Deliveries: You must not have any ongoing, uncompleted, or disputed trips in progress.
  • No Pending Payouts: For driver partners, all pending bank withdrawal transfers must be fully settled.
