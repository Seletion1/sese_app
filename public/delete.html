1. Your Right to Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your SESE account, profile, and all associated personal data at any time, in compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023 (NDPA) and Google Play Developer Policy. This right is unconditional and cannot be waived by platform terms.

2. How to Request Deletion

Option A — In-App (Instant)

  1. Open SESE Ride and sign in.
  2. Tap your profile icon → Settings.
  3. Scroll to Legal & Compliance → Tap Delete Account.
  4. Complete re-authentication (email/password or Google Sign-In).
  5. Confirm deletion. Active sessions terminate immediately.

Option B — Web Form (No App Needed)

  1. Scroll down to the Web Deletion Form on this page.
  2. Enter your registered phone number or email address.
  3. Select your account type and confirm consent.
  4. Submit. Our compliance desk processes within 24–48 business hours after identity verification.

You may also email support@sesetech.com from your registered address with the subject: Account Deletion Request — [Your Registered Phone Number]

3. What Data Is Deleted vs. Retained

CategoryData TypesAction & Timeline
Immediately Deleted Profile name, email address, phone number, profile photo, saved addresses, push notification tokens, in-app chat logs, voice recordings, delivery proof photos, Firebase Auth credentials (email/password & Google Sign-In link), FCM tokens, device identifiers, search history. Purged immediately upon deletion execution.
Hashed Identifiers SHA-256 hashed NIN, BVN, Driver's License, and phone number de-duplication records. Retained as one-way cryptographic hashes only to prevent fraudulent multi-account creation. Cannot re-identify you.
30-Day Regulatory Soft-Archive Completed trip receipts, fare invoices, commission ledger entries, wallet transaction records, driver payout summaries, dispute records. Held in an encrypted, access-restricted archive for 30 days for Nigerian AML, tax, and financial compliance. Permanently purged or irrevocably anonymized after 30 days.

4. Mandatory Prerequisites for Deletion

To protect your funds and prevent fraudulent account abandonment, your account cannot be deleted until ALL of the following conditions are met:

  • Zero Wallet Balance: Your in-app SESE Wallet balance must be exactly ₦0.00. Withdraw or spend any remaining balance first.
  • No Active Rides or Deliveries: You must not have any ongoing, uncompleted, or disputed trips or courier deliveries in progress.
  • No Pending Driver Payouts: (Driver accounts only) All pending bank withdrawal transfers must be fully settled before deletion can proceed.
  • No Active Compliance Flags: No unresolved fraud investigations or compliance holds flagged by SESE Compliance.

5. What Happens After Deletion

  • Your account is immediately deactivated and all login access is permanently revoked.
  • Your phone number and email address become eligible for new account registration after the 30-day soft-archive window.
  • You will receive a final confirmation email to your registered address confirming that deletion has been scheduled.
  • Any residual fully-anonymized analytics data (e.g., aggregate trip counts, route statistics) may be retained indefinitely in de-identified form for platform improvement — these cannot be used to re-identify you.
  • Deleted accounts cannot be reactivated. To use SESE services again, you must create a new account and complete full KYC re-verification.
