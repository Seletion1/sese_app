Privacy Policy & Data Safety Addendum

SESE TECHNOLOGY LTD ("SESE", "We", "Us") values your privacy. This policy details how we collect, store, encrypt, and process your personal and telemetry information across SESE Ride and SESE Hub in strict compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA), GDPR, and Google Play Data Safety Standards.

1. Android & iOS Permissions Disclosures

SESE explicitly discloses why each system permission is requested and how the corresponding data is processed:

a) Location Access (Foreground & Background)

  • Driver App (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION): Continuous location coordinates are collected when online, even when backgrounded or navigating via external maps. This enables accurate dispatch proximity, customer ETA calculations, live route tracking, and SOS safety monitoring.
  • Rider App: Location is gathered in the foreground while searching, calculating fares, or during active rides to accurately pinpoint pickup and drop-off points.

b) Contacts Access (READ_CONTACTS)

Accessed strictly on user tap when selecting "Book for Someone Else" or adding a parcel recipient. SESE launches the native system contact picker and retrieves only the selected contact's name and telephone number. SESE NEVER harvests, uploads, or stores your full contact address book.

c) Camera & Photos (CAMERA, READ_MEDIA_IMAGES)

Used solely for driver partner onboarding (identity documents, vehicle registration, and live facial verification selfie), courier dispute evidence (photographing damaged/missing goods), and profile picture updates.

d) Microphone / Audio (RECORD_AUDIO)

Required for peer-to-peer VoIP in-app voice calling between riders and drivers without exposing personal phone numbers, and for submitting voice messages to customer support.

e) Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW)

Allows drivers to receive immediate incoming ride alerts and navigate back to SESE Ride while utilizing third-party turn-by-turn navigation apps.

2. Data Security & Storage Standards

All sensitive information is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest using AES-256 encryption. Government identity numbers (NIN, BVN) and telephone numbers are normalized and stored as deterministic SHA-256 cryptographic hashes to prevent identity exposure and ensure strict one-account deduplication.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We work only with certified third-party service providers:

  • Payment Processing: Paystack (PCI-DSS Level 1 Certified). SESE never stores raw card credentials or CVV numbers.
  • Map & Geocoding Services: Google Maps Platform and OpenStreetMap raster tiles for route rendering and navigation.
  • Calling & Telephony: WebRTC and Agora RTC for encrypted in-app voice communication.
  • Cloud Infrastructure: Google Cloud Platform & Firebase for secure database, storage, and server-side compute.

4. Data Subject Rights & Account Deletion

Under the NDPA and GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, and delete your personal data. You may request account deletion at any time:

  • In-App: Go to Settings → Account Privacy → Delete Account.
  • Web Request: Visit our dedicated web deletion portal at https://sesetech.com/delete.

Upon request, all PII is permanently removed. Completed transaction logs and financial invoices are retained in a restricted 30-day soft archive for tax, financial, and regulatory auditing before final erasure.
