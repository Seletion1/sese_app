1. Compliance & Frameworks

SESE TECHNOLOGY LTD complies with applicable global data privacy frameworks including:

  • Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023 (NDPA) — enforced by the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).
  • Google Play Developer Data Safety Standards (Android).
  • Apple App Store Privacy Nutrition Labels (iOS).

2. Android & iOS Runtime Permissions

a) Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION)

  • Driver App: Continuous GPS tracking in foreground and background while online. Used for dispatch proximity matching, live route telemetry, accurate ETA computation, and SOS safety tracking.
  • Rider App: Foreground-only location to pinpoint pickup/drop-off pins and display nearby vehicles. Location is not tracked after trip completion.

b) Contacts (READ_CONTACTS)

Accessed strictly on explicit user tap for "Book for Someone Else" or recipient selection. SESE launches the native system contact picker and retrieves only the chosen contact's name and phone number. SESE NEVER harvests, uploads, or stores your full address book.

c) Camera & Photos (CAMERA, READ_MEDIA_IMAGES)

Used for driver KYC document uploads (license, vehicle papers, liveness selfie), damage claim photographs, proof-of-delivery images, trip/support chat image attachments, and profile picture updates.

d) Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO)

Used strictly for encrypted in-app WebRTC/Agora VoIP audio calling between riders and drivers, and voice note attachments in Support Chat only.

e) Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW) — Android Only

Enables the Driver Quick-Return Bubble floating overlay badge when a driver is online and the app is backgrounded. Requested exclusively after displaying a full prominent disclosure dialog, as required by Google Play policy. Toggleable by the driver at any time in Driver Settings.

f) Notifications & Locked-Screen Intents (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT)

Delivers real-time dispatch alerts, trip status updates, and payment confirmations. Full-screen dispatch alerts on locked screens are used exclusively to ensure online drivers don't miss incoming orders.

4. Data Encryption & Security Practices

  • Encrypted in Transit: All network communications between the mobile application and SESE backend servers use Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3).
  • No Data Shared with Third Parties: Data is processed solely by certified infrastructure processors under strict data processing agreements. No commercial advertising sharing occurs.
  • Account Creation Methods: Username (email/phone) and password, plus federated OAuth (Google Sign-In). All tokens are cryptographically secured by Firebase Authentication.
  • Identifier Masking: Government identification numbers (NIN, BVN, Driver's License) are stored exclusively as deterministic SHA-256 cryptographic hashes. Raw identity numbers are never persisted on SESE servers.
  • Fare Tamper Protection: Fares and financial calculations are signed with rotating 256-bit HMAC secrets to guarantee server authority.

5. Certified Third-Party Infrastructure Processors

  • Payment Processing: Paystack (PCI-DSS Level 1 Certified). SESE never stores raw card PAN or CVV numbers.
  • Maps & Geocoding: Google Maps Platform and OpenStreetMap tiles for route rendering and navigation.
  • Audio Calling: WebRTC (peer-to-peer audio) and Agora RTC (relay fallback) for encrypted in-app voice calling.
  • Cloud Infrastructure: Google Cloud Platform & Firebase (Firestore, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics).
  • Transactional Email: Resend API for administrative emails and OTP delivery.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Users have the absolute right to delete their SESE account and associated personal data at any time:

  • In-App: Settings → Legal & Compliance → Delete Account
  • Web Deletion Portal: https://sesetech.com/delete

In accordance with Nigerian financial anti-money laundering and tax regulations, financial ledger records, transaction receipts, and driver payout history are maintained in an access-restricted 30-day regulatory soft-archive before permanent, irreversible PII erasure.

7. Your Rights Under NDPA & GDPR

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023, you enjoy the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of personal information maintained by SESE.
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete profile data.
  • Erasure: Request permanent deletion of your account and personal records.
  • Restriction: Restrict processing of personal data under disputed circumstances.
  • Portability: Receive personal data in a structured, machine-readable format.

To exercise your rights, email our Data Protection Officer at: privacy@sesetech.com

8. Children's Privacy
