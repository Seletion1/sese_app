Privacy Policy & Google Play Data Safety Addendum

SESE TECHNOLOGY LTD ("SESE", "We", "Us") respects your fundamental right to privacy. This policy details how personal and telemetry information is collected, protected, and processed across SESE Ride and SESE Hub in strict compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023 and the Google Play Developer Data Safety Standards.

✓ Encrypted in Transit (TLS 1.3) ✓ No Data Shared with Third Parties ✓ Deletion Request Supported ✓ OAuth & Password Logins

1. Android & iOS Permissions Disclosures

SESE explicitly discloses why each system permission is requested and how the corresponding data is processed:

a) Location Access (Foreground & Background)

  • Driver App (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION): Continuous location coordinates are collected when online, even when backgrounded or navigating via external maps. This enables accurate dispatch proximity, customer ETA calculations, live route tracking, and SOS safety monitoring.
  • Rider App: Location is gathered in the foreground while searching, calculating fares, or during active rides to accurately pinpoint pickup and drop-off points.

b) Contacts Access (READ_CONTACTS)

Accessed strictly on user tap when selecting "Book for Someone Else" or adding a parcel recipient. SESE launches the native system contact picker and retrieves only the selected contact's name and telephone number. SESE NEVER harvests, uploads, or stores your full contact address book.

c) Camera & Photos (CAMERA, READ_MEDIA_IMAGES)

Used solely for driver partner onboarding (identity documents, vehicle registration, and live facial verification selfie), courier dispute evidence (photographing damaged/missing goods), proof of delivery images, and profile picture updates.

d) Microphone / Audio (RECORD_AUDIO)

Required for peer-to-peer VoIP in-app voice calling between riders and drivers without exposing personal phone numbers (via WebRTC/Agora RTC), and for submitting voice messages to customer support.

e) Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW) — Android Only

Enables the Driver Quick-Return Bubble floating overlay badge when a driver is online and the app is backgrounded. Displayed exclusively after an explicit prominent disclosure dialog. Drivers can toggle this off at any time in Driver Settings.

f) Notifications & Locked-Screen Intents (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT)

Delivers real-time dispatch alerts, ride progress updates, and payment confirmations to drivers and customers, including full-screen dispatch alerts on locked screens for online drivers.

3. Data Security & Storage Standards

All sensitive information is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest using AES-256. Government identity numbers (NIN, BVN) and phone numbers are normalized and stored as deterministic SHA-256 cryptographic hashes to prevent identity exposure and ensure strict one-account deduplication.

Account Creation Methods: SESE supports account registration via username (email/phone) and password, as well as federated OAuth (Google Sign-In). All authentication tokens are cryptographically secured by Firebase Authentication.

4. Certified Third-Party Processors

We partner only with certified service providers bound by strict data processing agreements:

  • Payment Processing: Paystack (PCI-DSS Level 1 Certified). SESE never stores raw card PAN or CVV numbers.
  • Map & Geocoding Services: Google Maps Platform and OpenStreetMap raster tiles for route rendering and navigation.
  • Calling & Telephony: WebRTC (peer-to-peer audio) and Agora RTC (relay fallback) for encrypted in-app voice calling.
  • Cloud Infrastructure: Google Cloud Platform & Firebase for secure database, storage, and server-side compute.
  • Transactional Email: Resend API for OTP delivery and administrative notifications.

5. Data Subject Rights & Account Deletion

Under the NDPA 2023 and GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, and delete your personal data. You may request account deletion at any time:

  • In-App: Go to Settings → Legal & Compliance → Delete Account.
  • Web Request: Visit our dedicated web deletion portal at https://sesetech.com/delete.
  • Email Request: Send an email to support@sesetech.com from your registered address.

Upon deletion, all profile PII, authentication credentials, and media files are permanently removed. Completed transaction logs and financial records are retained in an access-restricted 30-day soft archive for tax, financial, and anti-fraud auditing compliance before final erasure.
